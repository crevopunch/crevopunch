## Per-venue notes

**Binance USDⓈ-M.** The FAQ says BNB paid from the futures wallet gets a 10% reduction, and BNB must be moved from spot into the futures wallet. The first-tier perp fee was not confirmed directly; the spot-linked schedule snippet (0.02%/0.04%) is not the futures base. Funding cap/floor: for contracts at 25x or lower max leverage, ±3% (FAQ/2023 notice); for higher leverage, 0.75 × maintenance margin at max leverage. Default rate interest term is 0.01% per 8h, clamped ±0.05%. Some contracts have ±2% or ±2.5% caps. Use `GET /fapi/v1/fundingInfo` for the live per-symbol interval and caps. Max leverage on BTC and small caps and liquidation mechanics: UNVERIFIED (not gathered).

**Bybit.** Non-VIP futures fee is 0.02%/0.055% per two snippets. The fee page also showed a 0.018%/0.0495% row attributed to a regular discount, which I could not reconcile. Funding defaults to 8h (00/08/16 UTC). The cap rule is min((IM − MM) × 0.75, MM). A new formula, Funding = clamp[(avg premium + clamp(interest − premium, ±0.05%)) × N/8, caps], is phasing in from 2026-11-24 and scales to each contract's interval. Pre-market perps: 0.04% maker / 0.1% taker. Max leverage and liquidation: UNVERIFIED.

**OKX.** The OKB discount is not currently offered, per the OKX Trading Fee Rules FAQ. OKX's US fee framework uses volume tiers: Regular 0.200%/0.350%. Global base perp rates are UNVERIFIED. The funding default is 8h, with per-contract 1/2/4h intervals; the interval escalates when the rate hits a cap. Max leverage, withdrawal fee, and liquidation: UNVERIFIED.

**Kraken.** Kraken Pro tiers are based on the best of 30-day spot volume, 30-day futures volume, or assets on platform, and do not combine. Newly listed crypto perps have a separate 6-month schedule. Funding payments carry no trading fee. US derivatives use flat per-contract fees, but the page snapshot conflicts ($0.0113 vs $0.15), so that figure is UNVERIFIED. Funding interval, deposit/withdrawal fees, leverage: UNVERIFIED.

**Hyperliquid.** Base perps fee was taken from the app's portfolio page: 0.045% taker and 0.015% maker. Some markets (XYZ100, COST) show 0.009%/0.003%, which may be a different category or tier. Staking HYPE reduces trading fees; the staking discount percentages were not found, and the docs (not fetched) list the tiers. Builder codes let an approved builder charge a per-order fee, and users must approve each builder. Builder fee caps are UNVERIFIED. Withdrawal is $1 USDC; the deposit/withdrawal flow and bridge status are UNVERIFIED. Gas for trading is UNVERIFIED (HL's homepage describes HYPE as used for gas fees). Max leverage and liquidation: UNVERIFIED.

**Aster.** Official docs show 0% maker on USDT perps and a 5% discount when paying in ASTER. The taker rate is UNVERIFIED because sources range from 0.035% to 0.04%. USD1-margined perps show 0%/0.005%. Stock/RWA perp fees conflict across sources and are UNVERIFIED. The VIP program uses 14-day rolling volume and counts ASTER across spot, perp and staking balances. No separate staking-specific fee discount was found. Funding, withdrawal, leverage, and liquidation: UNVERIFIED.

**Lighter.** Standard accounts pay 0 maker and 0 taker per the official trading-fees page. Premium rates are UNVERIFIED because third-party sources disagree. LIT staking discount levels are UNVERIFIED. Funding is peer-to-peer with no exchange fee, and the hourly rate is clamped to ±0.5%. Gas: trading is effectively free on Standard; the gas/withdrawal note is UNVERIFIED. Max leverage and liquidation: UNVERIFIED.

**dYdX v4.** Entry tier is 0.010% maker and 0.050% taker, and the top tier (≥$200M, 30d) is -0.011% maker (rebate) and 0.025% taker. DYDX staking can discount taker fees but not maker rebates; the percentages are UNVERIFIED. Funding ticks hourly per the docs, with an 8h formula in a help article, so the interval is UNVERIFIED. Withdrawal goes dYdX → Noble → destination via Skip: $0.1–$7 plus the source-chain gas, minimum 11 USDC, and Noble USDC withdrawals are rate-limited to max(1% TVL, $1M)/hr by default. Deposits: CCTP for native USDC; Skip Go Fast at 10 bps plus source gas. Deposit fee figures conflict and are UNVERIFIED. Max leverage and liquidation: UNVERIFIED.
